# US Valuation Scan: Public Contract

## Purpose

Render a fast pressure-screen card from a canonical indicator artifact supplied by a private execution adapter.

This skill is not a data-acquisition implementation, SEC first-read workflow, full DCF, target-price model, fair-value claim, or investment recommendation.

## Input contract

The private adapter supplies:

- a verified US-listed `symbol`;
- an optional verified display name;
- a refresh or reuse decision;
- a canonical indicator artifact with calculation provenance;
- optional `receipt.review_summary` with the FY base, fiscal quarters, report dates, currency, and CFO/CapEx/interest base amounts, current-period additions, prior-period deductions, and TTM results for a brief pre-output check.

When a new receipt uses TTM, the adapter also supplies `receipt.fy_fallback`: a complete alternative FY receipt, including its own `presentation`, generated by the same canonical calculator. It is not a set of inputs for the agent to recalculate.

The skill must not rediscover the company or silently replace the supplied identity.

## Calculation boundary

One canonical calculator owns Owner FCFF, Operating EV, tax treatment, historical growth comparisons, and bounded reverse-DCF pressure diagnostics.

The renderer may read the canonical artifact and render its values. It must not recompute measures from raw statement rows or introduce a second formula.

FMP standardized statements are the calculation basis. For current cash-flow and interest components, the adapter uses `latest complete FY + sum(current fiscal-year Q1..Qk) - sum(prior fiscal-year Q1..Qk)`, where `k` is 1, 2, or 3 and FMP quarterly rows are single-quarter flows. Pair quarters by fiscal year and quarter, check their actual report dates, and use the same periods and currency across components. If the latest FY already covers the latest report period, use FY. Missing or conflicting duplicate required quarters, unconfirmed half-year records, or Q4 arriving before its FY fall back to the complete FY. Historical growth and annual tax-rate rules remain unchanged.

This is a rough valuation screen: accept small annual/quarterly differences without requiring four quarters to reconcile exactly to FY, hard discrepancy thresholds, or a filing-by-filing audit.

All monetary inputs used in one calculation must be in the canonical USD analysis currency. For a foreign issuer or ADR whose FMP statements retain a non-USD `reportedCurrency`, the private adapter must verify one statement currency and apply an FMP-sourced conversion consistently to every included statement and balance-sheet monetary value. The USD quote and market capitalization remain unconverted. Missing or inconsistent currency evidence prevents mixing those inputs; invalid quarterly currency evidence can retain an otherwise valid FY result. Profile `isAdr` alone is not routing or currency evidence.

The current adapter has no reusable result cache. A call therefore fetches live data whether `refresh` is true or false, and records `refresh_requested`, `refreshed`, and `data_mode` separately.

## Ready presentation

For a ready result, return `markdown_v1` and render every line in this order:

```text
{company_name}（{symbol}）
相对历史增长压力：{中文标签}

Owner FCFF：{一位小数} 亿美元
（{TTM或最近完整年度}，截至 {period_end}）
Operating EV：{一位小数} 亿美元
（市值截至 {market_data_date}）
Owner FCFF Yield：{一位小数百分比}

**隐含 5Y Owner FCFF CAGR：**
**{一位小数百分比}**

历史增长对照
- 营收 CAGR：3Y {百分比}｜5Y {百分比}
- NOPAT CAGR：3Y {百分比}｜5Y {百分比}
- Owner FCFF CAGR：3Y {百分比}｜5Y {百分比}

标签参考：{中文历史指标名称}（{百分比}）
隐含增速较标签参考：{高或低} {百分点绝对值} 个百分点
估值尺子：5 年显性期｜10% 折现率｜0% 永续增长
结果状态：ready。
```

Translate pressure labels as `light=较轻`, `explainable=可解释`, `stretched=偏高`, and `high_pressure=高压力`. Never expose internal keys such as `light`, `fcff_5y`, or raw decimal ratios in the card.

The frozen ruler is five explicit years, a 10% discount rate, and 0% terminal growth.

Amounts and dates must retain the canonical artifact's units and as-of basis. Format USD amounts in hundred-million USD units and all ratios as percentages with one decimal place.

## Non-ready presentation

`partial` and `not_assessed` use the same ordered card with `未形成` placeholders and the actual result status. Rejected and failed states use a concise neutral card. Detailed reason codes stay in the private receipt for diagnosis; the public card states only that no valuation conclusion was formed. Missing or unsolved inputs must not be converted into a valuation conclusion.

## Output boundary

The executor returns one `valuation_scan_terminal_receipt_v2` object. Normally use its main `presentation`. Only a clear period or unit error evidenced by `review_summary` permits selecting the complete `fy_fallback.presentation` instead; append one short reason and identify the FY basis. Never combine TTM numbers with an FY label or construct a missing fallback. Older receipts without a review summary retain their original presentation.

Internal paths, raw source payloads, annual series, credentials, runtime metadata, and the review summary are not part of the public presentation.

## Prohibitions

- No target price, fair value, or buy/sell conclusion.
- No second calculator.
- No direct service calls from this public contract.
- No SEC, transcript, or broader research expansion.

## Brief pre-output check

输出前，若有随附计算摘要，简要检查期间是否连续、同期是否对应、币种和单位是否一致，以及是否把全年或半年金额当成单季重复相加。默认采用 FMP 数据；小幅年季差异不触发年报审计，也不自行改数。大幅增长、零或负 TTM FCFF 本身不等于数据错误，不得为了得到更好看的结果改用 FY。仅在摘要给出明确期间或单位错误证据时，整体采用同一计算器提供的完整 FY 替代回执，追加一句原因并说明年度口径和实际截止日。不要混用两个回执的数字，不向用户展开内部摘要；没有摘要则沿用原 presentation。
